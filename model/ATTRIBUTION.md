# Surface model attribution

The cortical surface in this educational atlas uses the FreeSurfer `fsaverage5`
average pial surface distributed with Nilearn. It is an average template, not an
individual MRI reconstruction.

- FreeSurfer fsaverage: <https://surfer.nmr.mgh.harvard.edu/fswiki/FsAverage>
- Nilearn dataset interface: <https://nilearn.github.io/stable/modules/generated/nilearn.datasets.fetch_surf_fsaverage.html>
- Fischl B, Sereno MI, Dale AM. Cortical Surface-Based Analysis II: Inflation,
  Flattening, and a Surface-Based Coordinate System. *NeuroImage*. 1999.
- Nilearn is distributed under the BSD 3-Clause license. The local Three.js
  runtime is distributed under the MIT license; see `../vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt`.

The lobe colors in this atlas are approximate educational overlays based on
surface position. They are not a clinical parcellation or a substitute for an
anatomical atlas.
